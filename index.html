The Legacy of Jean-Vincent Bully

Founded in 1803 by master perfumer Jean-Vincent Bully, the Officine established its reputation across Europe with patented cosmetic formulations, including the iconic Vinaigre de Toilette[cite: 1].

Immortalesed by Honoré de Balzac in César Birotteau[cite: 1], the House was revived in 2014 by Victoire de Taillac and Ramdane Touhami[cite: 1], upholding 19th-century luxury, bespoke calligraphy, and officinal wisdom.

The 10 Commandments of Buly Appearance

Don't 👟

1. No Sneakers or Trainers

Footwear must strictly consist of clean, polished black leather town shoes or ankle boots[cite: 2].

Don't 🎨

2. No Flamboyant Tights

Avoid fluorescent or clashing tights[cite: 2]. Harmonious solid tones (navy, burgundy, white) or bare legs in summer.

Do 👔

3. Spotless White Collars

White collars and shirts must remain immaculate[cite: 2]. Wash with bicarbonate or percarbonate to preserve brightness.

Do 👗

4. Steam-Refreshed Skirts

The pleated navy-blue skirt of the Officière must retain its crisp pleats, free from creases or fraying[cite: 2].

Do 🧤

5. The Visible White Glove

Tucked subtly into the skirt or trousers waistband, the white glove remains ready for presenting items[cite: 2].

Don't 💇‍♀️

6. No Loose Hair Strands

Foreheads must remain clear. Hair must be secured smoothly back without falling forward[cite: 2].

Do 🌸

7. Natural Makeup & Nails

Makeup must highlight natural features delicately[cite: 2]. No false eyelashes[cite: 2]. Subtle single-color or sheer nail polish[cite: 2].

Do 🐢

8. Buly Tortoiseshell Accessories

Only official Buly tortoiseshell barrettes and hair pins are authorized for securing hairstyles[cite: 2].

Don't 🎽

9. No Visible Undershirts

When wearing the navy jumper, only the white collar should protrude. No visible shirt hemlines below[cite: 2].

Do ✨

10. Detail is You

“Detail is you and you are the detail.” Approach every interaction with genuine poise and quiet courtesy[cite: 2].

Hairstyle Tutorial: Curly & Coily Hair Profiles

1
Apply a hydrating balm to smooth the hairline while respecting natural texture.
2
Gather curls smoothly at the nape into a tight, low gathering point.
3
Twist and pin securely to maintain a neat, compact bun silhouette.
4
Affix the engraved Buly tortoiseshell barrette across the top of the bun.
Expected Final Result

Disciplined low bun with Buly barrette.

Hairstyle Tutorial: Very Sleek, Short & Long Profiles

1
Brush hair thoroughly backward from the forehead with absolute symmetry.
2
Twist lengths continuously into a low circular chignon at the nape.
3
Insert the Buly tortoiseshell pin diagonally to lock the hair firm.
4
Ensure no stray strands brush the face or collar.
Expected Final Result

Sleek chignon locked with Buly pin.

Japanese Spring Ritual: Fine Rice Powder & Tsubaki Camellia Glow
Shared by Officière Y., Kyoto Boutique • Theme: Facial Care & Cross-Selling

To celebrate spring in Kyoto, we recommend combining our gentle exfoliating Poudre de Riz with a few drops of pure Huile de Camélia du Japon (Tsubaki). This traditional recipe smooths skin texture effortlessly. Best sales cross-selling bridge: follow with a spray of Eau Superfine to lock in moisture and introduce the guest to our facial plant oils universe!
