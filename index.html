Summary: The Legacy Sales Ceremonial

History & Philosophy of the House

Honoring our Officières and Officiers in preserving universal beauty standards and French officinal excellence since 1803.

The Legacy of Jean-Vincent Bully

Founded in 1803 by master perfumer Jean-Vincent Bully, the Officine established its reputation across Europe with patented cosmetic formulations, including the iconic Vinaigre de Toilette.

Immortalised by Honoré de Balzac in César Birotteau, the House was revived in 2014 by Victoire de Taillac and Ramdane Touhami, upholding 19th-century luxury, bespoke calligraphy, and officinal wisdom.

The Buly Sales Ceremonial Steps

Every Officière (and Officier) guides our visitors through a theatrical, poetic, and attentive sales ritual:

1. The Welcome & Threshold

Greet every guest with a warm smile or nod. Ask if it is their first discovery of the Officine.

2. The Olfactory Discovery

Present our water-based perfumes (Eaux Triples) and pure plant oils with descriptive poetry.

3. Bespoke Artistry

Offer customized calligraphy on labels, embossing, and artistic gift packaging.

4. The Sovereign Farewell

Hand over the purchase with both hands, using white gloves and a tailored parting intention.

The Do's — Standards of Elegance

Do

Spotless White Collars

White collars and shirts must remain immaculate. Wash with bicarbonate or percarbonate to preserve brightness.

Do

Steam-Refreshed Skirts & Trousers

Uniform garments must retain crisp pleats and sharp lines, completely free from creases or fraying.

Do

The Visible White Glove

Tucked subtly into the waistband, the white glove remains visible and ready for presenting items with sovereign care.

Do

Natural Makeup & Nails

Makeup must highlight natural features delicately. Subtle single-color or sheer nail polish.

Do

Buly Tortoiseshell Accessories

Only official Buly tortoiseshell barrettes and hair pins are authorized for securing hairstyles.

The Don'ts — Prohibitions & Strict Limits

Don't

No Sneakers or Trainers

Footwear must strictly consist of clean, polished black leather town shoes or ankle boots. No casual sneakers.

Don't

No Flamboyant Tights or Socks

Avoid fluorescent or clashing hosiery. Harmonious solid tones (navy, burgundy, white) or bare legs in summer.

Don't

No Loose Hair Strands

Foreheads must remain clear. Hair must be secured smoothly back without falling forward or escaping the bun.

Don't

No Visible Undershirts

When wearing the navy jumper, only the white collar should protrude. No visible shirt hemlines below.

Don't

No False Eyelashes

False eyelashes and artificial heavy makeup disrupt the refined simplicity required by the Officine.
