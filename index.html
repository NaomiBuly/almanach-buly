History & Philosophy of the House

Since 1803, the pinnacle of universal skincare, poetic refinement, and the French art of living.

The Legacy of Jean-Vincent Bully

Founded in 1803 by the master perfumer and distiller Jean-Vincent Bully in the heart of Paris, the Officine quickly distinguished itself through revolutionary cosmetic inventions, notably the famous patented Vinaigre de Toilette[cite: 1], which earned it European renown.

Celebrated as the inspiration behind Honoré de Balzac's novel César Birotteau[cite: 1], the House was passionately reinvented in 2014 by Ramdane Touhami and Victoire de Taillac[cite: 1], preserving officinal expertise, bespoke calligraphy, and 19th-century luxury aesthetics.

The Buly Sales Ceremonial

In our boutiques, the theatrical staging of sales requires grace, sincerity, and absolute precision. Every female officer embodies the following ceremonial steps:

1. The Welcoming Threshold

Greet every customer who comes in with a word, a nod, or a warm look if busy[cite: 1]. Inquire gently: "Is this your first visit? Do you already know the Officine?"[cite: 1]

2. The Olfactory & Skincare Odyssey

Master scenarios: presenting UOB products for a first visit or a regular visit[cite: 1]. Emphasize water-based perfumes (Eau Triple) and natural plant oils.

3. Personalisation & Art

Offer bespoke calligraphy on labels, engraving, embossing, and exquisite gift wrapping[cite: 1]. Present each custom touch directly to the customer[cite: 1].

4. The Sovereign Farewell

Handle the bag with both hands, accompany the finish with a personalized intention, and bid farewell with sincere gratitude and a white glove[cite: 1].

Grooming & Uniforms

The 10 Commandments of Buly Appearance, Uniform Regulations, and Hair Texture Styling Protocols for Great Female Officers.

The 10 Commandments of Buly Uniforms & Appearance

Don't

1. No Fluorescent Tones

Never wear fluorescent shades or artificial sheens. Moderation always takes precedence[cite: 2].

Don't

2. No Sneakers or Trainers

Footwear must be in black leather, shoes or ankle boots, polished and formal[cite: 2]. No athletic shoes ever.

Don't

3. No Colored Tights Clashes

Tights may be colored (white, navy, burgundy, yellow, forest green)[cite: 2], but avoid jarring neon hues. Bare legs in summer are welcome.

Do

4. Impeccable White Collars

White collars and shirts must be spotless. Wash at 40°C to 60°C with bicarbonate of soda or percarbonate[cite: 2].

Do

5. The Pleated Skirt Regimen

The deep navy-blue pleated skirt must be free from creases and fraying. Refresh with steam to maintain pleats[cite: 2].

Do

6. The White Glove Display

The white glove must never be hidden or misplaced; tucked into the waistband, it remains visible as a sign of care[cite: 2].

Don't

7. No Loose Hair Strands

Hair must be kept in order, venturing not into the wind's caprices[cite: 2]. Foreheads must remain clear.

Do

8. Natural Makeup & Nails

Make-up must be light and natural. Nail polish should be sheer, natural, or a subtle single color (no false eyelashes)[cite: 2].

Do

9. Official Tortoiseshell Hair Accessories

Buly hair clips and barrettes form an integral part of the uniform, placed with discernment[cite: 2].

Do

10. Detail is You

“Detail is you and you are the detail.” Approach every shift with sovereign courtesy and professional poise[cite: 2].

Hair Styling Protocol: Curly & Coily Hair Profiles

Tailored for textured hair to ensure an immaculate, disciplined low bun while honoring natural volume.

1
Apply a smoothing balm to relax hairline frizz with gentle hydration.
2
Gather curls securely at the nape into a disciplined low base.
3
Pin tightly to prevent volume expansion around the temples.
4
Secure with the official Buly tortoiseshell barrette.

Hair Styling Protocol: Sleek, Short & Long Hair Profiles

For straight, long, or bobbed hair: absolute neatness with zero stray strands.

1
Part hair neatly and brush back with absolute symmetry.
2
Twist lengths gracefully into a compact low chignon.
3
Insert the Buly hair pin diagonally through the base.
4
Verify final elegance in the mirror before boutique opening.
